Flipbook Maker

Story Learning Lab: turn your pages into a flipbook you can share or embed

Layout

One page shows a single page at a time. Two pages shows an open book, with a left and a right page side by side.

Background

Wood table places the flipbook on a wooden table.

Page turn

Flip turns each page over its spine. Curl lets readers drag a corner, and the page bends and folds over, like a real book.

Choose images (JPG or PNG) or PDF files. Each PDF page becomes one flipbook page, in order. Pages are added in the order you select them. A PDF needs an internet connection the first time, to load its reader.

Starts on the first page turn and plays until it finishes. Page turns do not stop it, and readers can pause it. Up to 20 MB. For a long story, save the recording as MP3 at mono, 64 kbps, to keep the file small.
